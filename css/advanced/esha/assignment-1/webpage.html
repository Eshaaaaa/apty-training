Book Review: The Midnight Library

⭐ A Journey Between Life and Regret

“The Midnight Library” by Matt Haig explores the infinite possibilities our lives can take, wrapped inside a powerful story of self-discovery and second chances.

Nora Seed, the protagonist, finds herself in a mystical library between life and death where each book represents a different version of her life. With every decision she undoes, she experiences what could’ve been—new careers, lost loves, alternate futures—but none without their own flaws.

What makes the novel truly gripping is its gentle reminder that every life has meaning, even in its imperfections. Haig uses simple prose to explore complex emotional themes: depression, choice, and purpose. It’s both a comfort read and a wake-up call.

If you’ve ever wondered “What if I had…?”, this novel will feel like a mirror—one that helps you embrace the life you already have.

📘 Book Info

Author: Matt Haig

Genre: Fiction / Philosophy

Rating: ★★★★☆ (4.5/5)
